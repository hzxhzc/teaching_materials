Exit ticket

(a) Referring to one qualitative characteristic each, explain why a non-current asset is reported at Historical cost, and why it is also reported at Carrying value in the Balance Sheet.

(b) Explain the difference between Depreciation expense and Accumulated depreciation, and name the report each appears in.

(a) Historical cost: Verifiability (source document). Carrying value: Relevance (decision-making).
(b) Depreciation expense: amount consumed this year, Income Statement. Accumulated depreciation: total consumed to date, Balance Sheet.
