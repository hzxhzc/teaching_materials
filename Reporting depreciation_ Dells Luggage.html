5. Exit ticket

(a) Referring to one qualitative characteristic each, explain why a non-current asset is reported at Historical cost and why it is also reported at Carrying value in the Balance Sheet.

Historical cost:

Carrying value:

(b) Explain the difference between Depreciation expense and Accumulated depreciation, and name the report each appears in.

(a) Historical cost: Verifiability. It is the original purchase price, verifiable by a source document, so independent observers can check it. Carrying value: Relevance. Once the asset is depreciated, the Carrying value (the value yet to be consumed, plus any residual value) is more useful for decision-making than Historical cost.
(b) Depreciation expense is the amount consumed in the current reporting period, reported in the Income Statement. Accumulated depreciation is the total consumed over the asset's life so far, reported in the Balance Sheet.
